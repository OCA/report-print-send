This module adds printing groups to **base_report_to_printer**. A printing
group gathers users and the printers they work with, and carries the
specific printing actions of the reports for all its members, so the
printing behaviour is configured once per team instead of once per user.
With **base_report_to_printer_select**, the printers a member is asked to
choose from are narrowed down to the printers of their groups.
