Pingen.com is a paid online service. It sends uploaded documents by
letter post.

One can decide, per document / attachment, if it should be pushed to
pingen.com. The documents are pushed asynchronously.

The informations of the documents from pingen.com are updated through
webhook calls.

Configuration
=============

In *Settings > General Settings > Pingen*, set for each company:

- the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** of a Pingen API client (Pingen
  app, user profile, *API Access*). The token is requested with the
  ``letter`` scope only;
- the **Organization ID** (UUID) the letters are billed to;
- the **Address position**: the envelope window (``left`` or ``right``)
  where the recipient address is printed on the first page of the
  documents;
- **Staging**: tick it to use the separate staging environment
  (https://identity-staging.pingen.com, https://api-staging.pingen.com)
  with a staging account; nothing is printed there.

These credentials are only readable by administrators.

Webhooks are configured in the Pingen app, one per event category, all
signed with the **Webhook secret** entered on the company:

- issues: ``https://<your-odoo>/pingen/letter_issues``
- sent: ``https://<your-odoo>/pingen/sent_letters``
- delivered: ``https://<your-odoo>/pingen/delivered_letters``
- undeliverable: ``https://<your-odoo>/pingen/undeliverable_letters``

The routes must be reachable from the Internet. A call whose signature
does not match the secret of a company is rejected (HTTP 403).

Usage
=====

On the attachment view, a new pingen.com section has been added. You can
tick a box to push the document to pingen.com.

There is 3 additional options:

   - Send: the document will not be only uploaded, but will be also be
     sent
   - Speed: priority or economy
   - Type of print: color or black and white

Once the configuration is done and the attachment saved, a Pingen
Document is created. You can directly access to the latter on the Link
on the right on the attachment view.

You can find them in Pingen Documents App or in the more convenient
Documents menu if you have installed the document module.

Deleting a Pingen document also deletes its attachment unless it is
linked to another record.

Errors
------

Sometimes, pingen.com will refuse to send a document because it does not
meet its requirements. In such case, the document's state becomes
"Pingen Error" and you will need to manually handle the case, either
from the pingen.com backend, or by changing the document on Odoo and
resolving the error on the Pingen Document.

When a connection error occurs, the action will be retried on the next
scheduler run.

Dependencies
------------

   - Require the Python library
     `requests_oauthlib <https://github.com/requests/requests-oauthlib>`__
   - The address must be in a format accepted by pingen.com: the last
     line is the country in English or German.

Layout check
------------

Before uploading to Pingen, every PDF page is checked for content within
5 mm of any edge and in the lower-left 15 mm × 15 mm square. Detected
content blocks the upload and sets the document to "Pingen Error".
Correct the layout and create a new document. The postage area and
address window are not checked. If the PDF cannot be inspected, the
upload proceeds for Pingen to validate it.

Letter information before sending
---------------------------------

After upload, the document shows the address read by Pingen, destination
country, page count and estimated cost in the indicated currency. The
estimate uses the selected delivery and printing options; refresh the
letter information after changing them. Uploaded letters that have not
been sent yet are refreshed every hour. The actual cost appears after
sending.

A document uploaded with Send disabled is still sent by the scheduled
action *Run Pingen Document Push* (hourly) unless that action is
disabled. Use the *Ask pingen.com to send the document* button to send
it earlier.

Daily update
------------

The daily update also removes the documents whose letter was deleted on
Pingen, with their attachment unless it is linked to another record.

Tracking number and events
--------------------------

The letter's tracking number and events (validation, printing, handover
to the carrier and carrier steps) are stored on the document and shown
in the *Tracking* tab, newest first. They are read from Pingen at every
webhook, by the daily *Run Pingen Document Update* action (for sent
letters for 45 days after upload, with or without a tracking number),
and by the *Update the letter's informations* button. Pingen sends no
webhook for carrier steps and provides no tracking link.
